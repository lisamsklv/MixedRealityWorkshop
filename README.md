# Doom: Barista

A game made by Lizaveta Maskaliova (s2310238084) and Simon Saurer (s2310238062)

This barista simulator teaches you the interesting benefits of working in customer service: rotting in hell for eternity!
You find customers, who want some blood in their coffee or a milkshake with a brain and lungs. Try to serve as many of those smoky demons as possible to achieve the highest score!
